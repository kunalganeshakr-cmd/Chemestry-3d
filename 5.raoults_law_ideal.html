📌 Raoult's Law & Ideal Solution

  • • Raoult's Law: Partial vapour pressure of each volatile component is directly proportional to its mole fraction.
  • • Formula: Ptotal = PA°xA + PB°xB
  • • Interactions: A–B forces = A–A and B–B forces.
  • • Ideal Conditions: ΔHmix = 0 and ΔVmix = 0
  • • Examples: Benzene + Toluene, n-Hexane + n-Heptane.
