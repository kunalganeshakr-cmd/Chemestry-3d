📌 Degree of Dissociation (α)

  • • Definition: Fraction of total moles of electrolyte that dissociate into ions at equilibrium.
  • • Formula: α = (Moles dissociated) / (Total moles)
  • • Percent Dissociation: % Dissociation = α × 100
  • • Values: Strong (α ≈ 1), Weak (0 < α < 1), Non-electrolyte (α = 0).
