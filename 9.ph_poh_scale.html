📌 pH & pOH Scale Key Points

  • • pH Formula: pH = −log₁₀[H₃O⁺]
  • • pOH Formula: pOH = −log₁₀[OH⁻]
  • • Relation (at 298 K): pH + pOH = 14
  • • Scale: pH < 7 (Acidic), pH = 7 (Neutral), pH > 7 (Basic).
