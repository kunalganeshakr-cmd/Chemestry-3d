📌 Tonicity of Solutions (RBC)

  • • Isotonic (0.9% NaCl): Same osmotic pressure as blood plasma. No net flow → RBC keeps normal biconcave shape.
  • • Hypotonic (< 0.9% NaCl): Lower osmotic pressure outside. Water enters RBC (Endosmosis) → Cell swells & may burst.
  • • Hypertonic (> 0.9% NaCl): Higher osmotic pressure outside. Water leaves RBC (Exosmosis) → Cell shrinks (Plasmolysis).
