📌 Depression in Freezing Point (ΔTf)

  • • Freezing Point: Temp. where liquid and solid phases have equal vapour pressure.
  • • Why it drops: Solute particles interfere with the ordered crystal lattice formation of solvent.
  • • Formula: ΔTf = Tf° − Tf = Kf · m
  • • Kf: Cryoscopic constant (molal depression constant, K kg mol⁻¹).
  • • Uses: Antifreeze (ethylene glycol) in car radiators, salt on icy roads.
