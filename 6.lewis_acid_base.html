📌 Lewis Acid & Base Theory

  • • Lewis Acid: Electron-pair acceptor (empty orbital), e.g., BF₃, AlCl₃, H⁺.
  • • Lewis Base: Electron-pair donor (lone pair), e.g., NH₃, H₂O, OH⁻.
  • • Adduct: Donation forms a coordinate covalent bond (H₃N → BF₃).
