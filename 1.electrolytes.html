📌 Types of Electrolytes

  • • Strong Electrolyte: Ionizes almost completely (~100%) in water → High conductivity (Ex: NaCl, HCl, NaOH).
  • • Weak Electrolyte: Ionizes partially (<5%) → Low conductivity (Ex: CH₃COOH, NH₄OH).
  • • Non-Electrolyte: Does not ionize at all → No conductivity (Ex: Glucose, Urea, Sucrose).
