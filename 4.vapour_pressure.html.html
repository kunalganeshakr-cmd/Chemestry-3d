Vapour Pressure = P₁° (High)

📌 Vapour Pressure Key Points

  • • Vapour Pressure: Pressure exerted by vapour in dynamic equilibrium with its liquid at a given temperature.
  • • Pure Solvent (P₁°): Entire surface is occupied by volatile solvent molecules → High rate of evaporation.
  • • Solution (P₁): Non-volatile solute blocks part of the surface → Fewer molecules escape into vapour phase.
  • • Result: P₁ < P₁° (Lowering of Vapour Pressure: ΔP = P₁° − P₁).
