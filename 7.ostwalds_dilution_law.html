📌 Ostwald's Dilution Law

  • • Statement: For a weak electrolyte, degree of dissociation (α) is inversely proportional to square root of concentration (C).
  • • Formula: α = √(Ka / C) = √(Ka · V)
  • • Effect of Dilution: Adding water increases volume (V), decreases C, and increases α.
  • • Limitation: Valid only for weak electrolytes, not strong electrolytes.
