📌 Relative Lowering of V.P.

  • • Colligative Property: Depends only on the number of solute particles, not their nature.
  • • Formula: (P₁° − P₁) / P₁° = x₂
  • • Molar Mass (M₂): ΔP / P₁° = (W₂ · M₁) / (M₂ · W₁)
  • • Mechanism: Adding more non-volatile solute covers more surface area, proportionally reducing evaporation.
