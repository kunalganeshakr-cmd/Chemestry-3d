📌 Osmosis & SPM Key Points

  • • Osmosis: Spontaneous net flow of solvent molecules from pure solvent (or dilute solution) to concentrated solution through an SPM.
  • • SPM (Semi-Permeable Membrane): Allows only small solvent molecules to pass, blocking larger solute particles.
  • • Examples of SPM: Cellophane, parchment paper, copper ferrocyanide [Cu₂Fe(CN)₆].
  • • Result: Liquid level rises on the concentrated solution side.
