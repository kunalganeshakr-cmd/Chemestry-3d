📌 Acid-Base Titration & Indicators

  • • Neutralization: H⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → H₂O(l)
  • • Phenolphthalein (HPh): Colorless in acid (pH < 8.2) → Pink in base (pH > 10).
  • • Methyl Orange (MeOH): Red in acid (pH < 3.1) → Yellow in base (pH > 4.4).
  • • End Point: Sharp color change signals complete neutralization.
