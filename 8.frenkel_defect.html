📌 Frenkel Defect Key Points

  • • Type: Stoichiometric / Dislocation Defect
  • • Cause: Smaller ion (cation +) leaves its lattice site and moves to an interstitial site
  • • Density: Remains Unchanged (no ion leaves crystal)
  • • Condition: Large difference in size of cation & anion
  • • Examples: ZnS, AgCl, AgBr, AgI
