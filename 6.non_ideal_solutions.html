📌 Non-Ideal Solutions Key Points

  • • Positive Deviation: A–B forces are weaker than A–A & B–B. Molecules escape easily → Higher Vapour Pressure.
  • • +ve Properties: ΔHmix > 0, ΔVmix > 0 (Ex: Ethanol + Acetone, CS₂ + Acetone).
  • • Negative Deviation: A–B forces are stronger (e.g., H-bonding). Molecules held tightly → Lower Vapour Pressure.
  • • −ve Properties: ΔHmix < 0, ΔVmix < 0 (Ex: Chloroform + Acetone, H₂O + HNO₃).
