📌 Henry's Law Key Points

  • • Statement: Solubility of a gas in a liquid is directly proportional to the pressure of the gas over the solution.
  • • Formula: S = KH · P (KH = Henry's law constant)
  • • Unit of KH: mol L⁻¹ bar⁻¹
  • • Effect of Pressure: Higher pressure forces more gas molecules into the liquid.
  • • Examples: Carbonated soft drinks (CO₂ under high pressure), Scuba diving.
