H₂O + H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + OH⁻ (Kw = 10⁻¹⁴)

📌 Ionic Product of Water (Kw)

  • • Autoionization: Pure water self-ionizes slightly into H₃O⁺ and OH⁻ ions.
  • • Ionic Product: Kw = [H₃O⁺][OH⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴ at 298 K.
  • • Neutral Water: [H₃O⁺] = [OH⁻] = 10⁻⁷ M (pH = 7).
