📌 Common Ion Effect

  • • Definition: Suppression of ionization of a weak electrolyte by adding a strong electrolyte containing a common ion.
  • • Equilibrium: CH₃COOH ⇌ CH₃COO⁻ + H⁺
  • • Adding CH₃COONa: Increases [CH₃COO⁻] → By Le Chatelier's principle, equilibrium shifts backward → [H⁺] and α decrease.
