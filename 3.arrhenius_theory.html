Arrhenius Acid (HCl in H₂O)

12th HSC • Ch 3: Ionic Equilibria (3D Model)

H⁺ Ion (Hydrogen Ion)
Cl⁻ Ion (Chloride Ion)
HCl(aq) → H⁺(aq) + Cl⁻(aq)

📌 Arrhenius Theory Key Points

  • • Arrhenius Acid: Substance containing H that produces H⁺ ions in aqueous solution (Ex: HCl, H₂SO₄).
  • • Arrhenius Base: Substance containing OH that produces OH⁻ ions in aqueous solution (Ex: NaOH, KOH).
  • • Limitation: Applicable only to aqueous solutions; cannot explain bases like NH₃ (which lack OH⁻).
