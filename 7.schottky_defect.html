12th HSC • Solid State (3D Model)

Cation (+)
Anion (−)
Vacancy (Missing Ion)

📌 Schottky Defect Key Points

  • • Type: Stoichiometric / Vacancy Defect
  • • Cause: Equal number of cations (+) and anions (−) are missing from lattice sites
  • • Density: Decreases (mass decreases, volume same)
  • • Neutrality: Electrical neutrality is maintained
  • • Examples: NaCl, KCl, CsCl, AgBr
