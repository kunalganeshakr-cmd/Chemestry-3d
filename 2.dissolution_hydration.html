📌 Dissolution & Hydration Key Points

  • • Ion-Dipole Interaction: Polar water molecules attract Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions away from the crystal lattice.
  • • Hydration of Na⁺: Negative oxygen ends (δ⁻) of H₂O face toward Na⁺.
  • • Hydration of Cl⁻: Positive hydrogen ends (δ⁺) of H₂O face toward Cl⁻.
  • • Condition: Dissolution occurs when Hydration Enthalpy ≥ Lattice Enthalpy.
