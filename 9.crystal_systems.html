7 Crystal Systems

12th HSC • Solid State (3D Model)

1. Cubic
Edges: a = b = c
Angles: α = β = γ = 90°
Examples: NaCl, Cu, Diamond

📌 7 Crystal Systems (14 Bravais Lattices)

  • • Cubic: a=b=c, α=β=γ=90° (3 lattices)
  • • Tetragonal: a=b≠c, α=β=γ=90° (2 lattices)
  • • Orthorhombic: a≠b≠c, α=β=γ=90° (4 lattices)
  • • Hexagonal: a=b≠c, α=β=90°, γ=120° (1 lattice)
  • • Rhombohedral: a=b=c, α=β=γ≠90° (1 lattice)
  • • Monoclinic: a≠b≠c, α=γ=90°, β≠90° (2 lattices)
  • • Triclinic: a≠b≠c, α≠β≠γ≠90° (1 lattice)
