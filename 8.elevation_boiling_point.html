📌 Elevation of Boiling Point (ΔTb)

  • • Boiling Point: Temp. at which Vapour Pressure equals atmospheric pressure (1 atm).
  • • Why it rises: Non-volatile solute lowers V.P.; extra heat is needed so V.P. reaches 1 atm again.
  • • Formula: ΔTb = Tb − Tb° = Kb · m
  • • Kb: Ebullioscopic constant (molal elevation constant, K kg mol⁻¹).
  • • Molar Mass (M₂): M₂ = (1000 · Kb · W₂) / (ΔTb · W₁)
