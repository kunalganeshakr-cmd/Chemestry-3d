📌 Osmotic Pressure & RO Key Points

  • • Osmotic Pressure (π): Excess hydrostatic pressure applied on solution side to just stop osmosis.
  • • Formula: π = CRT = (n₂/V)RT
  • • Reverse Osmosis (RO): If external pressure P > π is applied on salt water, pure water flows out through SPM.
  • • Application: Desalination of seawater & home RO water purifiers.
