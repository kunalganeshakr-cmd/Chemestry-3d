F-Center (Metal Excess)

12th HSC • Solid State (3D Model)

Cation (Na⁺)
Anion (Cl⁻)
Trapped Electron (e⁻)

📌 F-Center Key Points

  • • Type: Non-stoichiometric (Metal Excess Defect)
  • • Cause: Anion (Cl⁻) leaves its site, and an unpaired electron (e⁻) occupies the vacancy
  • • Color Center: Imparts color to crystal (Farbenzenter)
  • • Examples: NaCl → Yellow, LiCl → Pink, KCl → Violet
  • • Magnetism: Becomes Paramagnetic
