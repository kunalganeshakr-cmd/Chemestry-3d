📌 Solubility Product (Ksp) & Precipitation

  • • Ksp Expression: For BxAy, Ksp = [By+]x[Ax−]y = xxyySx+y
  • • IP < Ksp: Unsaturated → More salt dissolves (No ppt).
  • • IP = Ksp: Saturated → Equilibrium established.
  • • IP > Ksp: Supersaturated → Precipitation occurs!
