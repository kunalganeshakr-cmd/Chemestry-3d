📌 Hydrolysis of Salts

  • • SA + SB (NaCl): No hydrolysis → Neutral (pH = 7).
  • • SA + WB (NH₄Cl): Cation hydrolyses (NH₄⁺ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄OH + H⁺) → Acidic (pH < 7).
  • • WA + SB (CH₃COONa): Anion hydrolyses (CH₃COO⁻ + H₂O ⇌ CH₃COOH + OH⁻) → Basic (pH > 7).
  • • WA + WB (CH₃COONH₄): Both ions hydrolyse → pH depends on Ka & Kb.
