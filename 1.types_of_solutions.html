📌 Types of Solutions Key Points

  • • Solution: Homogeneous mixture of 2 or more substances (Solute + Solvent).
  • • Solid Solution: Solvent is solid. Particles are tightly packed & vibrate in place (Ex: Brass, Bronze, Steel).
  • • Liquid Solution: Solvent is liquid. Particles slide/flow past each other (Ex: Salt in water, Ethanol in water).
  • • Gaseous Solution: Solvent is gas. Particles move freely & rapidly (Ex: Air - N₂ & O₂).
